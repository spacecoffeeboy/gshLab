# ALMENGI — White & Playful

## Direction
White canvas, black text and black primary buttons. Inspired by bold independent studio typography and restrained sticker accents. Product clarity comes before decoration.

## Tokens
- Background/card: #FFFFFF; subtle fill: #F6F6F6.
- Foreground/primary action: #111111; inverse text: #FFFFFF.
- Secondary text: #666666; borders: #E5E5E5.
- Single accent color: lime #D8F36A. Former lavender surfaces use neutral gray; do not introduce purple accents.
- Accent colors appear only on small tags, selected options and decorative stickers. No large colored backgrounds.
- Typography: locally bundled Space Grotesk 600 for headings; Inter 400/500/600 for body; JetBrains Mono for small indexes.
- Heading size: 44px desktop, 30px mobile; card titles 28px/22px; body 15px with 1.6 line height.
- Cards: 16px radius, thin borders. Buttons: pill or 10px radius. No gradients, glass, backdrop blur or hard shadows.
- Spacing: 4/8/12/16/20/24/32/48/64/96px.

## Interaction and responsive layout
- Main journey: landing CTA → By topic / Random / Saved hub → Quiz or Reveal.
- Desktop: top navigation and two-column feed. Mobile: single column and fixed bottom navigation with safe-area clearance.
- Black primary CTA with white label, outlined secondary controls. Touch targets >=44px.
- Selection uses pale lime fill plus black outline and a selection symbol, never color alone.
- Reveal requires selection followed by explicit Reveal action. No right/wrong or score.
- Save controls persist locally; publication restrictions remain unchanged.
- No animated decorative loops. Respect reduced motion. Visible black focus outlines.

## Imagery
Simple black line illustrations on neutral backgrounds. At most one small lime sticker per card. No fake photos, ratings, source verification or statistics.
